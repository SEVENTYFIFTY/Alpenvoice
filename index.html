01 — Executive Summary

The opportunity in one page

Petrus AI is a Swiss-Portuguese deep-tech venture building the first behavioural early-warning system for children's mental health. The platform is a working tool for the professionals who carry the duty of care — psychologists, psychiatrists, school counsellors, paediatricians, sport psychologists. It detects pattern shifts two to four weeks before a crisis becomes visible, and surfaces them with explainable, time-stamped evidence to the qualified clinician in a position to act.

Petrus AI is deployed through schools, clinical services, sports formation centres, and healthcare providers — but in every deployment, the user is the professional. We are not a chatbot. We are not a diagnostic tool. We are the layer between observation and intervention that does not currently exist.

02 — The Problem

Children are signalling.
The system is not listening.

Mental-health distress in children rarely arrives as a single event. It arrives as a slow shift in behaviour over weeks. The shift is observable — but it is observed in fragments, by people who do not share notes, and by the time it reaches a clinician, the situation has typically already escalated.

04 — The Solution

An early-warning system,
not a diagnostic tool

Petrus AI is a behavioural intelligence platform. It collects multi-source data on child wellbeing through age-appropriate interactions, identifies pattern shifts against a personalised baseline, and surfaces them — with full explainability — to a qualified adult who is in a position to act. The clinical decision remains human. Always.

What Petrus AI does

  • Age-adaptive interaction. Different interface paradigms for ages 6–8 (playful tasks), 9–12 (mood journals), 13–18 (anonymous structured check-ins).
  • Multi-source data fusion. Behavioural, contextual, longitudinal — and, where consented, biometric proxies via existing devices.
  • Personalised baseline. Each child is benchmarked against their own history, not against a population norm.
  • Explainable alerts. Every alert ships with a ranked contributor list, time-window visualisation, and confidence score.
  • Cross-environment bridge. Home and school context combined for the first time, with role-based privacy controls.

What Petrus AI does not do

  • Not a diagnostic tool. Petrus does not give DSM/ICD labels. It surfaces signal, never diagnosis.
  • Not a replacement for a clinician. Every alert routes to a qualified professional; the platform does not act.
  • Not surveillance. The child is the user, not the subject. All design choices are vetted for child safeguarding.
  • Not a chatbot companion. We do not build relationships with children. We build a thin observational layer with explicit consent.
  • Not a replacement for human relationships. Teachers, families, clinicians remain primary. Petrus is infrastructure.

Architecture pillars

Layer 1

Interaction Engine

Age-adaptive front-end. Co-designed with child psychologists. Web and tablet. Offline-capable. Multi-language: DE, FR, IT, RM, EN, PT.

Layer 2

Detection Engine

Gradient-boosted ensemble model. SHAP-based feature attribution. Personalised baselines. Threshold calibration tuned to clinical cost asymmetry.

Layer 3

Alert & Explainability

Human-readable contributor breakdown. 28-day time-window visualisation. Confidence score. Full audit trail for clinical review.

Six modules, one platform

The detection layer is invisible to the child. From the child's point of view, the platform is a structured space for self-expression — six modules, each clinically grounded, each producing observational signal that flows to the professional dashboard.

Module Clinical description Value to the professional
Mood Circle Daily emotional-state register — simple, non-invasive entry point Longitudinal mood data; pattern and cyclicity identification
Dynamic Sociogram Visual mapping of relationships: friends, family, pets, zones of proximity and distance Relationship evolution over time; isolation and conflict signals

The "167 Hours" Principle

A child sees their therapist one hour per week. They live the other 167 hours without continuous clinical observation. Petrus AI captures structured signal during brief, voluntary, age-appropriate interactions spread across the week (target: 3–5 short interactions per child per week, total ~30–60 minutes). What is different is that those interactions are longitudinal, structured, and visible to the clinician — converting episodic observation into temporal patterning.

Privacy & safeguarding by design

  • Swiss-hosted infrastructure with edge-computing for behavioural raw data; only derived signals leave the device.
  • GDPR + Swiss FADP compliant by architecture, not by retrofit. Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) led by Swiss legal counsel.
  • EU AI Act conformity roadmap aligned with high-risk-system requirements (children, sensitive health context).
  • Explicit, layered consent: child assent, parental consent, school authorisation. Renewable annually.
  • Right to deletion and portability built into the data model from day one.
  • No data sale, ever. Anonymised research access requires institutional ethics approval and aggregated outputs only.

05 — Scientific Innovation

What is genuinely new

The funding case for Petrus AI is not "we are building an app." It is that we are producing a defensible scientific contribution at the intersection of developmental psychology, behavioural data science, and explainable AI — a contribution that has commercial deployment as its natural distribution channel.

Innovation 1

Personalised Behavioural Baselining

Most behavioural-detection models compare individuals to population norms. Children are too heterogeneous for that to work. Petrus AI builds a per-child longitudinal baseline within the first weeks of engagement — a methodology adapted from neuropsychological assessment.

Innovation 2

Cross-Environment Signal Fusion

We fuse home-context and school-context signals through a privacy-preserving role-based access model. To our knowledge, no deployed product currently does this in the child wellbeing setting.

Innovation 3

Clinically Grounded XAI Architecture

Every alert is accompanied by ranked SHAP-style contributors translated into clinically meaningful language. The explainability layer is a co-design with practising psychologists, not a post-hoc retrofit.

Innovation 4

Longitudinal Child Mental-Health Dataset

Anonymised, consented, ethics-approved. The first dataset of its kind in the European context. A scientific public good with commercial license value to research institutions, public-health bodies, and pharmaceutical research.

Expected scientific outputs (2026–2028)

  • 2 peer-reviewed publications: methodology paper (signal taxonomy) + validation study results
  • 1 conference paper at a Tier-1 venue (ICML / NeurIPS / EMNLP or a developmental psychology venue)
  • 1 master-thesis / PhD-thesis topic supervised through FH Graubünden and Universidade de Aveiro
  • 1 dataset release (anonymised summary statistics) under controlled access
