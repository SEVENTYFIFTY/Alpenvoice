        Excel

        Keep your project list in Excel? Download the template, fill in the Team, Projects, Phases and Drawings sheets, and upload it. Re-uploading updates existing rows; nothing is deleted.

        Download template Export everything (.xlsx)

        Google Drive

        Add a Google Sheet (or an .xlsx stored in Drive) that uses the same layout as the Excel template. It is pulled automatically on a schedule, or on demand with “Sync now”.

          Gmail

          Each person connects their own mailbox. Atelier then shows threads with each project's clients and consultants, flags the ones waiting on a reply, and saves phase-change emails as drafts in your Gmail. It reads sender, subject and date only, never message text, and it never sends anything itself.

            Team

            Give each person access, then send them their invite link. Team can update projects; Viewer can only look; Principal can also manage the team and integrations.

              Wall displays

              A secret link for the office screen: open it once in the TV's browser and it stays signed in, read-only. Revoke it if the link leaks or the screen is replaced.
