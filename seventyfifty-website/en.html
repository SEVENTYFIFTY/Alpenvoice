Frequently Asked Questions

Straight Answers

Why no monthly fees?

Because you shouldn't rent your own operations. We quote the project, build it, train your team, and hand you the keys — you own the systems and the data. Ongoing support is available when you want it, shaped around your needs, never as a forced subscription.

Will our customers actually like talking to AI?

They already text, email and check FAQs. AI gives instant answers to simple questions — and complex requests still go to your empowered staff. Nothing changes for guests who prefer the phone or the front desk.

Won't our staff feel threatened?

Only if it's positioned wrong. This liberates staff from repetitive work to do what requires expertise and personality. In our experience, staff become the biggest advocates within a month.

What if the AI makes mistakes?

It will — rarely. We monitor continuously, update weekly, and build in safety measures and human hand-off points. Within months, AI accuracy exceeds human consistency for routine inquiries.

How long until we see results?

Weeks 1–3: your staff notices breathing room. Weeks 4–6: first measurable upselling increase. Months 2–3: revenue improvements are clear and consistent. Month 4+: the new normal — better service, better revenue.

Who is this for?

Primarily Swiss hotels of 50–200 rooms, restaurants, and SMEs with repetitive operational tasks — anyone who wants efficiency and revenue growth without adding headcount.
